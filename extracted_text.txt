Overview of GIS USERS and Scientific Scope
Aperçu et Portée Scientifique
Geographic Information Systems (GIS) currently occupy a strategic position in the analysis, management, and valorization of spatial data. They provide advanced tools that help understand the complexity of territorial dynamics and support decision-making processes. In a context characterized by climate change, accelerated urbanization, and increasing pressure on natural resources, GIS represents a key instrument for sustainable territorial governance. In Morocco, the use of GIS has experienced significant expansion across public, semi-public, and private sectors. 
Within this context, the International GIS Users Conference (FEZ GIS-Users’2026) has emerged as a scientific and technical platform dedicated to the promotion and dissemination of advances in geomatics. Since its first edition held in 2012 at the Faculty of Multidisciplinary Studies of Taza, followed by subsequent editions in Meknes (2014), Oujda (2016), Meknes (2018), Fez (2023) after an interruption caused by the COVID-19 pandemic, and Errachidia (2024), this event has contributed to building a dynamic national community that remains strongly open to international collaborations. 
The conference aims to promote innovation in emerging fields such as remote sensing, spatial modeling, Geo-Artificial Intelligence (Geo-AI), and geospatial Big Data. It also encourages interdisciplinarity and fosters interactions among researchers, decision-makers, and socio-economic stakeholders. Thus, the GIS-USERS Conference serves as a strategic framework for skills development, knowledge transfer, and the implementation of innovative solutions addressing current and future challenges of sustainable development.
